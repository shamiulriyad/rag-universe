# RAG vs Fine-tuning

> **Status:** Planned (Phase 2) · **Level:** Beginner
>
> This page is a placeholder. It lists the planned scope so contributors and learners know what is coming. The lesson content has not been written yet.

## Planned Learning Objectives

After this lesson, you will be able to:

- Explain what fine-tuning changes (model weights) and what RAG changes (model input).
- Compare the two on knowledge freshness, cost, source attribution, data privacy, and behaviour change.
- Explain why the two approaches are complementary rather than mutually exclusive.
- Choose an approach, or a combination, for a given scenario.

## Planned Outline

1. Three ways to adapt an LLM: prompting, retrieval, and fine-tuning.
2. What fine-tuning is good at (style, format, task behaviour) and what it is less suited to (frequently changing facts).
3. What RAG is good at (fresh, citable, access-controlled knowledge) and what it is less suited to.
4. Side-by-side comparison table.
5. Combining both approaches.
6. A decision guide with worked scenarios.
7. Summary and exercises.

Claims about relative cost or accuracy in this lesson will be backed by cited sources or clearly labelled as general engineering guidance.
