# Books

Books that support the RAG-Universe learning path. Free online editions are linked where the authors or publishers provide them.

**Inclusion criteria:** the book is written by recognized authors in the field, it is relevant to at least one module, and the link points to an official source (the author's, university's, or publisher's site). Links were checked on 2026-11-12.
